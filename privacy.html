Privacy Policy

桌面工坊 · 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 21 日 · 适用于 macOS App「桌面工坊 DeskCraft Widgets」

支持English

一句话总结：桌面工坊没有账号系统、没有广告、没有分析 SDK，也不会上传你的组件内容。所有组件配置、便签、待办、相册和文件默认只保存在你的 Mac 上。只有在你主动使用天气、定位、日历、提醒事项或音乐组件时，App 才会在获得系统授权后读取相应数据。

1. 我们处理哪些数据

  • 组件与布局数据：组件类型、位置、主题、便签/待办内容、相册索引、应用快捷方式等，保存在本机 App 数据目录中。
  • 文件收纳：你拖入的文件会移动到你所选择的收纳文件夹。App 只保存安全书签和文件夹名称，不读取与组件无关的文件。
  • 相册：你选择或拖入的图片会复制到本机 App 数据目录，用于轮播显示。
  • 位置：只有当你启用“使用当前位置”并授权定位时，App 才读取粗略位置，用于向 Open-Meteo 请求本地天气。位置不会保存到服务器，也不会用于广告或追踪。
  • 日历与提醒事项：只有在你授权后，App 才读取近期日程和提醒，用于「日程」组件；数据不会离开你的 Mac。
  • 音乐：只有在你安装并授权自动化后，App 才通过 AppleScript 读取 Apple Music / Spotify 的当前曲目和播放状态，并发送播放/暂停等控制命令；不会上传歌曲信息。
  • 购买：订阅和终身购买由 Apple StoreKit 处理。App 只读取交易校验结果和到期时间，不接触你的支付卡信息。
  • 跨设备同步（可选）：如果你在设置中启用「跨设备同步」，App 会把桌面快照写入你选择的 iCloud Drive 文件夹中的 DeskCraftWidgets-Sync.json。快照包含组件布局、场景、外观和偏好，可能包含便签/待办正文；不包含会员状态、文件收纳目录或相册原图。该文件存放在你的 iCloud Drive 中，由 Apple iCloud 按你的账户设置同步，开发者无法访问。
  • 诊断信息（可选）：「帮助与反馈」中的诊断信息只包含版本、组件类型/数量、权限状态和日志尾部，不包含便签/待办正文；由你主动复制或导出，不会自动上传。
  • 日志：App 会在本机记录有限的运行日志，用于排查问题；日志不会自动上传。

2. 数据如何共享

我们不出售、不出租、不共享你的个人数据。以下服务仅在对应功能中按需使用：

  • Open-Meteo：接收城市名或粗略坐标，返回天气预报。请参阅 Open-Meteo 的隐私政策。
  • Apple：StoreKit 购买、iCloud Drive/系统服务（如你使用）由 Apple 按其隐私政策处理。跨设备同步文件由你的 iCloud 账户承载，开发者不运营同步服务器。
  • Spotify / Apple Music：播放控制通过本机 Apple Events 完成，不经过我们的服务器。

3. 权限与你的选择

位置、日历、提醒事项、通知和自动化权限都可以在系统设置 → 隐私与安全性中随时关闭。关闭后对应组件会降级或显示无权限提示，不影响其他组件。文件收纳目录可在 App 设置中重新选择。

4. 数据保留与删除

数据保留在你的 Mac 上，直到你删除它。你可以在 App 内使用“重置全部数据”清除组件、布局、相册索引和本地缓存；也可以直接删除 App 及其数据目录。文件收纳中的文件不会被自动删除，需要你在 Finder 中处理。若启用了跨设备同步，停止同步或删除 iCloud Drive 中的 DeskCraftWidgets-Sync.json 即可移除云端快照。

5. 儿童

桌面工坊不是面向 13 岁以下儿童设计的服务，也不会主动收集儿童个人信息。

6. 安全

App 使用 macOS App Sandbox、用户选择文件授权和 Apple 的签名/公证机制保护本地数据。没有任何系统能保证绝对安全，请妥善保护你的 Mac 登录账户。

7. 政策更新与联系

如果本政策发生重大变化，我们会更新本页面并修改生效日期。问题请通过 支持页面 联系我们。

DeskCraft Widgets · Privacy Policy

Effective: October 21, 2026 · for the macOS app “DeskCraft Widgets”

In short: DeskCraft has no account system, no ads, and no analytics SDK. Widget settings, notes, to-dos, albums, and files stay on your Mac. The app reads location, calendars, reminders, or music only when you use those widgets and grant the corresponding macOS permission.

Data we process

  • Widget and layout data (types, positions, themes, notes, to-dos, album indexes, app shortcuts) is stored locally.
  • Files you drop into File Tray are moved to the folder you choose. Only a security-scoped bookmark and folder name are stored.
  • Photos you add are copied into the app’s local data directory.
  • Location is read only when you enable “Use Current Location” and grant permission; coarse coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo solely to fetch weather. They are not stored on our servers or used for advertising/tracking.
  • Calendars and reminders are read locally only after permission, for the Schedule widget. They are not uploaded.
  • Music: with automation permission, the app reads the current track/playback state from Apple Music or Spotify and sends playback commands locally. Track information is not uploaded.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit. The app only reads verified transaction results and expiration dates; it never sees payment card details.
  • Cross-device sync (optional): if you enable “Cross-Device Sync”, the app writes a desktop snapshot to DeskCraftWidgets-Sync.json in an iCloud Drive folder you choose. The snapshot contains widget layouts, scenes, appearance, and preferences, and may include note/to-do text; it does not include membership status, File Tray folders, or original photos. The file lives in your iCloud Drive and is synced by Apple iCloud under your account; the developer cannot access it.
  • Diagnostics (optional): diagnostics in Help & Feedback contain only version, widget types/counts, permission status, and a log tail—no note/to-do text. They are copied or exported by you and are never uploaded automatically.
  • Logs are written locally for troubleshooting and are not uploaded automatically.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data. Open-Meteo receives a city name or coarse coordinates to return a forecast. Apple processes StoreKit purchases and, if you enable sync, iCloud Drive storage; the developer does not operate a sync server. Playback control is performed locally via Apple Events.

Your choices

You can revoke location, calendar, reminders, notifications, and automation permissions at any time in System Settings → Privacy & Security. You can choose a different storage folder in the app. Disabling a permission only affects the related widget.

Retention and deletion

Data remains on your Mac until you delete it. Use “Reset All Data” in Settings to remove widgets, layouts, album indexes, and local caches, or delete the app and its data directory. Files in your chosen storage folder are not deleted automatically. If sync is enabled, stop syncing or delete DeskCraftWidgets-Sync.json from iCloud Drive to remove the cloud snapshot.

Children

DeskCraft is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Security

The app uses macOS App Sandbox, user-selected file access, and Apple code signing. No system can guarantee absolute security; protect your Mac account.

Changes and contact

We will update this page and the effective date if this policy changes materially. Contact us via the support page.
